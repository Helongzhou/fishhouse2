## English

### Working title

**Aquarium Journal**

Tagline: **Start with sand. Stay for the life inside.**

### Short description

Build a freshwater aquarium from sand, stone and living plants. Establish the biofilter, introduce fish and shrimp, then move closer to watch them explore, feed and settle into a world shaped by your care.

### About this game

An empty aquarium. A handful of sand. Your first stone.

Aquarium Journal is a single-player 3D freshwater aquascaping game about building a tank and enjoying the life that grows inside it. Start with a 60 cm aquarium and a guided triangle layout. Shape the scene with stones and low-tech plants, fill it with water, and establish the biofilter before introducing your first animals.

**Build with a purpose.**

Attach Anubias barteri var. nana, Microsorum pteropus and Taxiphyllum barbieri 'Bogor Moss' to your hardscape. Leave open swimming space and sheltered corners. Learn how placement and planting methods change your composition.

**Give your aquarium time to settle.**

Check water conditions, light and filtration. Complete a fishless cycle check before adding Ember Tetra, then gradually welcome Red Cherry Shrimp and Neon Tetra. Clear explanations help you decide what to adjust when conditions need attention.

**Move closer and watch.**

Follow individual fish as they turn, pause and search for food. Watch shrimp linger over a grazing spot. A sudden change in light may send some animals into cover; approaching the glass may bring curious fish toward a familiar feeding area. Reactions vary between individuals and change with their recent experiences.

**Care at your own pace.**

Feed your animals, change water and observe the results. Compress the aquarium calendar while the biofilter develops, or pause and take a look around. Save locally and continue later. Your aquarium does not deteriorate while the game is closed.

### Initial scope

- One 60 cm freshwater aquarium and a guided triangle aquascape.
- Three low-tech plants, stones, optional driftwood, a light and a hang-on-back filter.
- Ember Tetra, Neon Tetra and Red Cherry Shrimp, introduced in stages.
- Aquascaping, fishless cycling, feeding, water care and close observation.
- Single-player play and local saves. Launch interface languages will be confirmed after localization QA.

## 简体中文

### 暂定标题

**水草缸日记**

宣传语：**从一把底砂开始，为缸里的生命停留。**

### 短描述

从底砂、石头与水草开始，亲手布置一缸淡水景。建立生物过滤，分批迎来鱼虾，再把镜头推近，看它们探索、觅食，逐渐适应这个由你照看的小小世界。

### 关于这款游戏

一只空缸，一把底砂，第一块石头。

《水草缸日记》是一款单机3D淡水草缸布景养成游戏，关于亲手开出一缸水，也关于停下来看里面的生命。从一只60厘米鱼缸和三角入门布景开始，摆好石头与低技术水草，注水并建立生物过滤，再迎来第一批生物。

**每一次摆放都有意义。**

把矮水榕、铁皇冠和爪哇莫丝附着在硬景上，留出游动空间和安静的躲避处。了解不同的种植方式，让每片叶子成为构图的一部分。

**给新缸一点稳定下来的时间。**

检查水质、灯光和过滤。完成无鱼氮源挑战后加入火焰灯，再逐步迎来樱花虾和霓虹灯。环境需要调整时，清晰的说明会帮助你决定下一步。

**把镜头推近，看看它们。**

跟随不同个体的转向、停顿和觅食，看虾在一小块表面驻留。灯光突变时，有些生物可能躲起来；靠近玻璃时，好奇的鱼可能来到熟悉的投喂区。它们的反应因个体而异，也受近期经历影响。

**按自己的节奏照看。**

投喂、换水，观察这些操作的结果。等待生物过滤建立时，可以压缩游戏日历；也可以暂停，换个角度细看。本地保存，之后继续。退出游戏期间，鱼缸不会自行恶化。

- 分批加入火焰灯、霓虹灯和樱花虾。
- 布景、无鱼稳缸、投喂、基础水质养护和近距离观察。
